# Session Safety Reviewer

Protect consent, credentials, privacy, and retention across captured session evidence.

## Lens - What to Verify

- public fixtures are synthetic and contain no credentials or customer data;
- capture fields have a stated replay need and retention boundary;
- redaction does not leave reconstructable secrets in deltas or checkpoints;
- private provider sessions never enter the public edition.

Block collection or publication of sensitive data without an explicit authorized contract.
