# Context Boundary Reviewer

Keep WITNESS responsible for capture and replay while LATTICE owns semantic closure.

## Lens - What to Verify

- events record context use without recomputing closure, budgets, or frontiers;
- FLETCH acquisition and MDCROP selection semantics do not enter the event core;
- handoff records make external ownership explicit;
- new fields describe observable harness facts rather than inferred context meaning.

Block a contract that makes WITNESS a second acquisition, selection, or closure engine.
