## Integration

- ROLES: `.roles/ROLE.md` routes runtime completeness, LATTICE boundary,
  session safety, and provider portability concerns to matching reviewers.
- VTRACE: this public core does not yet carry repo-local `docs/vtrace/` rows;
  PITFALL cites role docs, maintenance policy, wave records, tests, and fixtures
  until trace records are added.
- Tests: workspace tests, retained CLI proof, JSON status/replay smokes,
  formatting, strict clippy, and PITFALL validators are the executable evidence
  hooks.
